The Boyband Zodiac

Is there any color trend across the twelve zodiac signs?

Each wedge is a zodiac sign, read off the members' birth dates — a wider wedge means more boyband members were born under that sign. The five rings inside a wedge show that sign's most common hair, eye, skin, shirt and pants color. Hover a ring for the exact split behind its color, or click a wedge to see its actual members.

How to read the rings

  • 1Hair color
  • 2Eye color
  • 3Skin tone
  • 4Shirt color
  • 5Pants color

Each ring's color is the real value, not a mapped one — there's no legend to decode. Hover a ring to see the exact split behind it, or click a wedge to open its member list.
