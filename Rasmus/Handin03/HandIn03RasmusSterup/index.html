Superficial Boyband Visualizer 3000

How alike do boyband members dress and look, and in which colour?

How to read it: each dot is a band, in its signature colour: the colour most of its members wear (or have, for hair, eyes and skin). A line connects the dot to where that colour sits on the wheel.

The further a dot is from the wheel, the more alike the band: at the outer ring (100%) every member has the signature colour. Overall outfit is the average of the shirt, jacket and bottom scores.

White, grey and black have no hue, so they have their own white-to-black wheel. Both wheels are shown, each on half of the circle; untick Colours or Greyscale to give the other one the whole circle. The wheel is stretched where the bands are: every shown band gets the same slice of the circle, so a colour with many bands gets a wide block in that colour, and colours with no bands shrink to a thin strip. Use the slider on the left to zoom in on part of the wheel.

Colours

Data: boys.csv, the boyband dataset (234 member records, 55 bands). Signature colour = the most common recorded colour; ties go to the colour that is more common in the whole dataset. Uniformity = the share of all band members with the signature colour. An empty jacket colour means the member wears no jacket. The 8 shirts recorded as "patterned" have no colour and count as not matching.
