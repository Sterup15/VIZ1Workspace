      How to read the chart

      Matching pairs

      Matching score = matching pairs / all known-member pairs

      0%: all known colours differ. 100%: all match. Shai's two black and two navy-blue bottoms give 2 matching pairs out of 6, or 33.3%.

      Missing colours are excluded. Fewer than two known colours means unavailable, not zero.

      Colours and scale

      Each band has one pin in its most-common-colour sector; ties go in the striped mixed sector. Arc width counts bands. The slider filters these same groups and follows the ring clockwise. Blended shades are not measured shades. Black and navy blue stay distinct; matching "patterned" categories need not mean identical patterns.

      Read dots against the labelled score circles, not the full spoke length. Filtering preserves positions; changing clothing rebuilds the sectors and clears incompatible filters or selections.

      Hue organises categories; it does not make the data cyclic. A straight dot plot would support more precise numerical comparisons. Recorded outfits do not establish styling intentions or causation.

      Source: the course-provided boyband dataset. 234 member records, not necessarily 234 distinct people.
